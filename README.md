## Projects

- **Transcriptomic Immune Panel for Predicting Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Response**: 47-gene XGBoost immune classifier trained on 8,542 TCGA PanCancer Atlas samples across 33 cancer types, with SMOTE for class imbalance and external validation on the IMvigor210 cohort (348 metastatic bladder cancer patients treated with atezolizumab).
- **Cancer Genomics Database Integration**: pipeline that cleans and integrates breast and colorectal cancer mutation data from cBioPortal, IntOGen, OncoVar, and Bharat Cancer Genome Atlas.
- **Drug Repurposing of GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Parkinson's Disease** (ongoing): computational pipeline from candidate selection to ADMET-type property analysis.
- **Multi-Epitope Vaccine Design Against HSV-1**: B-cell and T-cell epitope prediction with IEDB, antigenicity, allergenicity, and toxicity screening, molecular docking, and immune simulation.
